# Thunai — Tamil Scheme Guide

## Who it is for

People in Tamil Nadu and across India who need to find government health, medical-support, and education schemes — especially those who are not comfortable navigating English-only government portals, and those who may not know about lesser-known schemes they qualify for.

## What it does

The user explains their situation in Tamil or English. Thunai understands their need, asks only the necessary follow-up questions (one at a time), and suggests relevant Central Government, Tamil Nadu Government, or other verified India-wide schemes.

For each scheme, Thunai explains:
1. What the scheme provides, in plain language
2. Who may be eligible and which details still need confirmation
3. Whether it is Central, Tamil Nadu, or other, and where it applies
4. Likely documents needed, clearly marking anything that must be verified
5. The official application or information link and the next practical step
6. The official source and when the information was checked

## Why it matters

Millions of people in Tamil Nadu and India miss out on schemes they are eligible for because information is scattered across portals, often in English, and eligibility rules are complex. Thunai lowers that barrier by meeting people in their language — natural, friendly Tamil — and guiding them to the right scheme without overwhelming them.

## What makes it different

Natural, respectful Tamil conversation — not stiff bureaucratic Tamil or literal translations. The agent supports code-switching, transliteration, and English when the user does. Safety is code-owned: emergency phrases trigger a hard stop, and scheme facts (URLs, names, amounts) are enforced by code, not left to the model.
